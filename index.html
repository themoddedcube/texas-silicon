About

Hands-on silicon, start to finish

Members learn through an apprenticeship modeled on a real chip design flow, from architecture and RTL through synthesis, place and route, and signoff, using both industry and open-source tools. The goal is practical experience that carries straight into semiconductor internships and full-time roles.

Teams

Four teams, one flow

Every member joins one technical team and works alongside experienced leads.

01

Architecture

Workload analysis, performance modeling, and system specification.

02

RTL Design

SystemVerilog implementation of accelerator blocks.

03

Physical Design

Synthesis, place and route, timing closure, and signoff.

04

Design Verification

Testbenches, reference models, and formal equivalence checks.

Projects

What we're building

Our work centers on hardware for running AI models efficiently on small devices, built on open hardware standards like RISC-V.

Flagship

Reef NPU

An AI inference accelerator for on-device language models, built by all four teams.

Accelerator blocks

KV cache & attention

Hardware for compressing and managing the memory that transformer models use while generating text.

Open-source silicon

130nm tapeouts

Smaller test chips on the open SkyWater 130nm process, so members see a design all the way through to fabrication.
